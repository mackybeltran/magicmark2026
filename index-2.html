The One-Eyed Wonder

Magic born
from resilience

Outdoor Street Indoor Venue Festival Headliner 44 Countries Filipino–Canadian

In 2016, while on tour and chasing his dreams, Mark Beltran was violently assaulted and lost the sight in one eye. Most people would have gone home. Mark went back on stage.

He turned the eye patch into a signature, the trauma into a story, and the story into a show that has since played in 44 countries across four continents — from cobblestone squares in Poland to circus-tent venues in Belgium, from the streets of Switzerland to the festival stages of Canada.

Trained at the McBride Mystery School in Las Vegas and a former teaching assistant at the School of Busking in Cardiff, Mark blends world-class sleight of hand with raw crowd comedy — building circles of 100+ strangers into a single roaring audience, then detonating the finale with a blindfolded bullwhip.

A Filipino-Canadian performer with something real to say. The show travels anywhere and needs almost nothing to run.

The Show

⏲
Duration
30–60 minutes
30-minute sets through to full 60-minute shows with hat pass finale. Adaptable to programmed festival formats.
🏠
Format
Outdoor & Indoor
Performs in the round on any flat surface or on a stage. Outdoor minimum 6m × 6m. Fully adaptable to indoor venues.
🎤
Technical Rider
Minimal
Radio mic preferred (own equipment available). No lighting or rigging required. Self-contained. Setup: 20 minutes.
🌎
Languages
English · Adaptable
Performed in English with universal physical comedy. Proven with non-English speaking audiences across Europe and Asia.
👪
Audience
All ages · All-weather
Family-friendly with adult wit. Builds circles of 100–300 from a standing start. Suited to high foot-traffic festival environments.
💵
Fee
From €800
European: from €800 + accommodation + meals. Canadian: from $1,200 CAD. Multiple shows per day available.
